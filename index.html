How we ranked them

A dating profile isn't one photo. Hinge shows six, Tinder people swipe through several, and you'll discard plenty of generations that look slightly off. So the ranking weighs three things in this order: whether the photos are of your face (a trained personal model, not a filter on one selfie), how many usable photos the money buys, and whether you're locked into a subscription for something you need once.

What makes an AI dating photo get swiped right

  • One clear face shot first. Eye level, natural light, a real smile. The first photo decides most swipes.
  • Show a life, not a backdrop. Two or three scenes that hint at what you do on a Saturday beat six portraits.
  • Keep it you. Same hair, same build, same age. Your date should recognise you at the door.
  • Mix in one real photo with friends or a pet, so the profile doesn't read as all-AI.
  • Check the app's rules. Short version for the biggest one: are AI photos allowed on Tinder?
